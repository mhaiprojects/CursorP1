# Toxic Rain Pathfinder

**Budget:** ~10–30 chaos to map comfortably  
**Playstyle:** Ranged DOT mapper, kiting, safe  
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Why This Build

Toxic Rain (TR) has been a staple budget mapper for years. You fire pods that deal chaos DOT in an area—no weapon damage needed. Pathfinder gives flask sustain and ailment immunity. Works on a 4-link with self-found rares.

## Core Skills

| Slot | Gem | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Body | Toxic Rain | Main skill |
| Links | Vicious Projectiles → Efficacy → Withering Touch → Void Manipulation | 5-link; Mirage Archer on 6-link |
| Movement | Flame Dash | Faster Attacks optional |
| Auras | Grace, Defiance Banner | Purity of Elements if resists are low |
| Utility | Withering Step, Blood Rage, Malevolence (optional) | Malevolence for more DOT |

## Ascendancy (Pathfinder)

1. Nature's Boon  
2. Master Alchemist  
3. Nature's Adrenaline  
4. Plaguebringer

## Key Passive Tree Goals

- Life and resistances on Ranger path
- Chaos damage, damage over time, and projectile nodes
- Herbalism, Claws of the Magpie
- Point Blank is **not** used (TR is not attack damage)
- Pick up bow nodes only if using bow for +gem levels

## Gear (Budget → Upgrade)

### Weapon

**Early:** +1–2 to gem level bow (any rare or unique).  
**Budget unique:** Quill Rain (fast attacks for Mirage Archer) or any +gem bow.  
**Upgrade:** +1/+2 gem bow with attack speed.

### Body Armour

**Early:** Rare with life + resists.  
**Upgrade:** Queen of the Forest or rare 6-link.

### Quiver

Rare with life, resists, and damage over time multiplier (nice to have, not required).

### Flask Setup

1. Divine Life Flask  
2. Quicksilver (of Adrenaline)  
3. Granite or Jade  
4. Amethyst  
5. Silver or Basalt

### Optional Uniques

| Item | Why |
|------|-----|
| Witchfire Brew | Chaos damage flask (strong mid-game) |
| Dying Sun | Slightly larger pod area |
| Asenath's Gentle Touch | Gloves for clear (explosion on kill) |

## Defences

- 4k+ life for maps; evasion from Grace + gear
- Withering Step gives phasing and chaos res
- Stay mobile—TR rewards kiting
- Cap resists; chaos res helps against DOT

## Leveling Tips

1. Levels 1–12: Caustic Arrow or Split Arrow  
2. Level 16+: Toxic Rain with Mirage Archer when you get a 4-link  
3. Mirage Archer shoots pods while you run—huge QoL  
4. Do not invest in weapon elemental damage; all damage is gem-based

## Upgrade Path

1. 4-link → 5-link with Void Manipulation  
2. +2 gem bow  
3. Cluster jewels (Chaos DOT, Flasks)  
4. 6-link with Awakened gems (late endgame)

## PoB Notes

Import a "Toxic Rain Pathfinder" template from community sites. Ensure Mirage Archer is linked and that you are not accidentally scaling attack damage on gear.
