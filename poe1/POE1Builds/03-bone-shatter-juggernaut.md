# Bone Shatter Juggernaut

**Budget:** ~5 chaos (literally rares from drops)  
**Playstyle:** Face-tank melee, slam, high armour  
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Why This Build

Bone Shatter Juggernaut is the ultimate "bonk" league starter. It needs no uniques, scales with armour and endurance charges, and is forgiving for new players. Juggernaut gives physical mitigation, endurance charges, and unstoppable feel.

## Core Skills

| Slot | Gem | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Body | Bone Shatter | Main skill |
| Links | Pulverise → Brutality → Melee Physical Damage → Ruthless | 5-link; Fortify or Multistrike |
| Movement | Leap Slam → Faster Attacks | Fortify on hit via Leap Slam |
| Auras | Determination, Defiance Banner, Precision (if accuracy needed) | Pride optional for bossing |
| Utility | Molten Shell, Blood Rage, War Banner | Enduring Cry for endurance charges |

## Ascendancy (Juggernaut)

1. Unstoppable  
2. Undeniable  
3. Unbreakable  
4. Unflinching

## Key Passive Tree Goals

- Life and armour wheels from Marauder start
- Resolute Technique or accuracy for hits (Bone Shatter cannot crit by default—lean into non-crit)
- Physical damage, melee damage, warcry nodes
- Endurance charge duration and max charges
- Armour, block (if using shield), life regeneration

## Gear (Budget → Upgrade)

### Weapon

**Early:** Any high physical DPS mace (1h + shield or 2h).  
**Budget:** Rare siege axe or jagged maul with high flat phys.  
**Upgrade:** Exquisite Blade, Kongor's Undying Rage, or rare with +1 melee gems.

### Body Armour

**Early:** Highest armour rare you find.  
**Upgrade:** Rare with life, armour, and resists; aim for 6-link.

### Shield (if 1H)

High armour/life rare with block chance. Lioneye's Remorse or The Surrender are luxury upgrades.

### Flask Setup

1. Divine Life Flask  
2. Granite Flask (of Iron Skin)  
3. Basalt Flask  
4. Quicksilver  
5. Diamond or Sulphur (if using Enduring Cry)

## Defences

- Stack armour: Determination + Granite + Basalt + armour gear
- Juggernaut physical reduction is core scaling
- Endurance charges from Enduring Cry or kills
- 5k+ life target for maps
- Fortify from Leap Slam or Fortify support
- Molten Shell on left click for extra buffer

## Leveling Tips

1. Levels 1–12: Ground Slam or Cleave  
2. Level 28+: Bone Shatter when available  
3. Pick up all life nodes; armour is your damage reduction  
4. Use Enduring Cry whenever available for charges  
5. Resolute Technique avoids accuracy headaches

## Upgrade Path

1. 5-link with Brutality + Melee Phys  
2. Higher phys weapon  
3. Cluster jewels (Attack Damage while Holding Shield, Life)  
4. Titan's Heart or similar armour-stacking (optional late game)
