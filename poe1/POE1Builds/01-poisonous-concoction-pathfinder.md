# Poisonous Concoction Pathfinder

**Budget:** ~5–20 chaos to start, scales to endgame  
**Playstyle:** Fast mapper, strong single target via poison/DOT  
**Difficulty:** Easy

## Why This Build

Poisonous Concoction (PC) is one of the strongest league starters in POE1. It needs almost no uniques, clears screens with flask throws, and poison stacking gives excellent boss damage. Pathfinder provides flask uptime, ailment avoidance, and poison proliferation.

## Core Skills

| Slot | Gem | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| Body | Poisonous Concoction of Bouncing | Main skill; use Bouncing variant |
| Links | Awakened Unbound Ailments → Vicious Projectiles → Volatility → Lifetap | 5-link; add Efficacy or Deadly Ailments |
| Movement | Flame Dash | Or Frenzy for on-hit flask charges |
| Auras | Grace, Defiance Banner, Herald of Agony (optional) | Determination if armour-stacking |
| Utility | Molten Shell, Vitality, Withering Step | Withering Step for boss damage |

## Ascendancy (Pathfinder)

1. Nature's Boon  
2. Master Alchemist  
3. Nature's Adrenaline  
4. Plaguebringer (or Avatar of the Veil for more defence)

## Key Passive Tree Goals

- Life wheel on Ranger start (path toward Shadow for poison nodes)
- Herbalism, Claws of the Magpie, Dirty Techniques
- Poison and chaos damage clusters
- Life, elemental resistances (cap at 75%), armour or evasion
- Flask effect and duration nodes near Pathfinder

## Gear (Budget → Upgrade)

### Weapon

**Early:** Any +1–2 gem level wand or sceptre (craft or buy for 1–5c).  
**Upgrade:** +1 all spell skill gems sceptre with open suffix.

### Body Armour

**Early:** Rare evasion armour with life + resists.  
**Upgrade:** Queen of the Forest (evasion, speed) or rare with high life.

### Flask Setup

1. Divine Life Flask (instant recovery)  
2. Quicksilver Flask (of Adrenaline)  
3. Granite or Jade Flask  
4. Amethyst Flask (chaos res)  
5. Silver Flask or Dying Sun for clear

Pathfinder keeps flasks up almost permanently.

### Important Uniques (Optional)

| Item | Why |
|------|-----|
| Berek's Grip (rings) | Life leech + chill/shock synergy |
| Atziri's Promise (flask) | Chaos res + damage (mid-tier upgrade) |
| Dying Sun | Extra projectiles for clear |

None are required to complete white/yellow maps.

## Defences

- Aim for 4k+ life early, 5k+ maps, 6k+ endgame
- Cap elemental resists; grab chaos res where cheap
- Molten Shell + life flask panic button
- Pathfinder ailment avoidance reduces freeze/poison on you

## Leveling Tips

1. Levels 1–12: Caustic Arrow or Explosive Trap  
2. Level 12+: Switch to Poisonous Concoction when quest rewards it  
3. Prioritise gem levels; PC scales hard with gem levels  
4. Act 6+: grab second PC gem for off-hand if dual-wielding charges

## Upgrade Path

1. 5-link body armour  
2. +1 gem sceptre  
3. Cluster jewels (Chaos Damage over Time, Flask)  
4. Forbidden Flame/Flesh for extra ascendancy (luxury)
